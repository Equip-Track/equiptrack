About the project

About EquipTrack

EquipTrack is a front-end prototype of a corporate IT Asset Management (ITAM) portal, created as the final project for ITC 122 – Introduction to Web Design at Arellano University (ETEEAP BSCS).

The problem

Many companies still track equipment in spreadsheets or logbooks. Devices go missing, returns are late, warranties expire unnoticed, and audits take days.

Our solution

One web portal that covers the whole asset lifecycle: registration, borrowing, monitoring, depreciation and disposal, all accessible from any device.

Who it's for

IT departments, admin and facilities teams, and employees of small to large organizations who borrow or are issued company equipment.
