Policy

Equipment Borrowing Policy

  1. Only regular employees with a valid Employee ID may borrow equipment.
  2. Short-term borrowing is limited to 14 days; long-term assignments to 90 days, renewable.
  3. Requests must be filed at least one working day before the borrow date.
  4. The borrower is responsible for the asset until IT marks it as returned.
  5. Lost or damaged equipment must be reported to IT within 24 hours.
  6. Do not install unlicensed software or remove asset tags.
  7. Company data must be saved to the shared drive, not the local disk.
  8. Repeated overdue returns may suspend borrowing privileges for 30 days.
FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Who can see my request?

Only you, your department head and the IT asset team. Personal data is handled under the Data Privacy Act of 2012.

Can I extend my borrowing period?

Yes. Ask IT before the due date and they can extend it by 7 days from the Monitor page, if no one else has reserved the asset.

What if the asset I need is not available?

Pick a similar asset from the Inventory, or ask IT to notify you when it's returned.

What happens to old equipment?

It goes through the Retire & Dispose stage: secure data wipe, then redeployment, donation, or e-waste recycling.

Does EquipTrack work on my phone?

Yes. The portal is fully responsive and works on phones, tablets and desktops.
