Why EquipTrack

Built for how corporate IT really works

Spreadsheets get lost and stickers peel off. EquipTrack keeps one source of truth for every laptop, projector, phone and server your company owns.

Asset Register & Tagging

Every device gets a unique asset tag (like ET-LPT-0001), with specs, location, owner and purchase details in one record.

Borrow & Assignment Requests

Employees request equipment online. They pick an available asset, set dates, and get a ticket number instantly.

Real-time Monitoring

A live dashboard shows what's out, who has it, and what's due or overdue, with one-click returns and CSV export.

Lifecycle & Depreciation

Know each asset's book value and when it should be refreshed, using straight-line depreciation.

Warranty & Maintenance Alerts

Expired warranties and devices in repair are flagged so IT can act before users are affected.

Secure Disposal & Audit Trail

Every action is logged. Retired assets go through data wiping and proper e-waste disposal.
