Responsible disposal options

Redeploy

Move still-usable devices to staff with lighter workloads or training rooms.

Donate

Give wiped devices to partner schools and NGOs as part of CSR programs.

Certified Data Wipe

Erase drives securely and keep a certificate to comply with the Data Privacy Act of 2012.

E-waste Recycling

Send broken units to DENR-accredited e-waste recyclers instead of the trash.
